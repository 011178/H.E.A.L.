Dignity is a right.

Through Project H.E.A.L. (Hygiene, Empower, Aid, Love), high school student Aaliya Srivastava Jain is aiming to lead by example and support younger kids by providing basic hygiene items to them, regardless of their family’s financial situation. The 2025 Child and Family Well-Being Data ranked Georgia 37th for economic well-being (from 32nd the year before), with 18% of children living in poverty. This means more students are coming to school without basic essentials, leading to 'school avoidance' and hygiene-related bullying. Aaliya's project aligns with this problem by providing the direct health interventions needed to keep students in class and focused on learning.
