H.E.A.L.

Aaliya Srivastava Jain is addressing hygiene insecurity by creating Project H.E.A.L. (Hygiene, Empower, Aid, Love). Her plan is to remove financial and social barriers by integrating "Hygiene Intervention Packs" directly into the school’s social programs. This makes getting essentials a normal, stress-free part of the student experience.
